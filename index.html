The headline

Fewer sales don't automatically mean collapsing prices.

October brought fewer closed transactions, even as the metro-wide median sale price was relatively stable. The story is more nuanced than a simple buyer's- or seller's-market label.

The practical takeaway

Buyers have choices. Positioning matters.

More available inventory means presentation, competitive pricing, condition, and visibility all matter. We'll monitor your direct competition rather than rely on one metro-wide number.

05 / Pricing philosophy

The launch price is a strategy, not a guess.

We won't decide on a number until we've walked through the home, considered the condition and layout, verified square footage, and assessed the most relevant competitive properties.

Scenario A

Priced to compete

Positioning the home as an attractive option against its direct competition may increase early attention and the chances of stronger negotiation leverage.

Scenario B

Priced for a premium

A premium can be supported by condition, features, location or presentation—but buyers must recognize the additional value.

Scenario C

Priced beyond the market

When buyers don't see the value, prolonged exposure can lead to price reductions and less favorable negotiating dynamics.

What still needs to be verified

Reported square footage differs between 1,320 and 1,680 square feet. We have not yet toured the home or verified its finished area, room functionality, improvements or condition. Those details can materially affect comparative analysis and any price recommendation.

This presentation is a market and marketing strategy preview, not an appraisal or final comparative market analysis.

07 / A modern marketing advantage

Your home deserves a digital presence of its own.

Most listings live within someone else's platform. Our custom property page gives each home a standalone destination designed for broader discovery and a richer buyer experience.

Custom listing page

A home for your home's story.

Original property descriptions, neighborhood context, professional media and relevant search language—all brought together on a dedicated page on GoFreedle.com.

  • Search-friendly, original property content
  • Professional photography and available video or virtual tours
  • Features and lifestyle context beyond basic MLS fields
  • Direct destination for our social, video and email marketing
Search is changing

Built for Google and the AI-search era.

Buyers increasingly explore homes with conversational searches about locations, layouts and lifestyle needs. Clear, original property information can help search systems understand and potentially surface a listing.

No website can guarantee indexing, rankings, AI-generated recommendations or buyer traffic. Our goal is to increase the ways the property can be discovered.

The portal wars

Visibility shouldn't depend on any one portal.

As brokerages, MLS organizations and major real estate platforms debate listing distribution, exclusive inventory and display policies, buyers cannot always assume every search platform offers the exact same inventory. We favor broad exposure and an independently controlled home for the listing story.

Our approach: broad MLS distribution where available, major consumer home-search sites, social and video promotion, and a dedicated property page that isn't dependent on a single portal's presentation.

08 / Your launch timeline

A coordinated runway to launch day.

The best launches begin before a property becomes active. We'll work backward from the target launch date to create a smooth, well-prepared rollout.

1
Approximately 1 month before launch

Professional staging consultation

A professional stager visits the home and advises what to put away, how to arrange and decorate rooms, how to prepare for photographs, and what to do to keep the home ready for showings.

2
Approximately 2 weeks before launch

Professional photography

Schedule and complete photography once the staging plan is in place. Assemble the visual assets and confirm the strongest features to emphasize in our property story.

3
Approximately 1 week before active

Coming-soon marketing

Begin the coming-soon rollout, when permitted under applicable MLS rules, with an intentional message and an upcoming launch date.

4
Launch week

Custom listing blog and property page

Publish the home's dedicated GoFreedle.com property page and a coming-soon listing blog during launch week. Align digital content, photography and online sharing ahead of the active-listing debut.

5
Active launch

Go live, distribute and respond

Activate the MLS listing, coordinate exposure across home-search sites and marketing channels, manage showing logistics and monitor the earliest buyer response.

6
Each Tuesday after launch

Report, interpret and adapt

Deliver a consistent market and marketing update, assess activity against competing inventory and recommend thoughtful adjustments when the evidence calls for them.

Exact dates depend on photography availability, home readiness, seller preferences and applicable MLS/coming-soon policies. Additional media and promotional channels are tailored to the property.

09 / Communication commitment

Every Tuesday, you'll know where we stand.

We don't simply launch a listing and wait. Each Tuesday you'll receive a clear update on how your property is performing and what is changing in the market around it.

Your weekly Tuesday report includes
  • Online views and buyer engagement
  • Showing activity and useful buyer feedback
  • Competing properties that went under contract
  • Properties that sold and their sale prices, when available
  • New listings and competing options hitting the market
  • Mortgage interest-rate updates
  • Other economic and housing factors influencing demand
  • Our interpretation and any recommended next steps
More than reporting

We turn market activity into a plan.

If your home isn't selling right away, we'll continuously compare its exposure and buyer response against changing competition, market conditions and feedback.

That assessment helps us determine whether to stay the course, change how we present the home, strengthen promotion, or make a price adjustment when needed.

You're never left guessing what we're seeing or what we think should happen next.

10 / From interest to closing

The strategy doesn't stop when an offer arrives.

Once buyers engage, we help you evaluate the full agreement and guide the process through closing with a focus on your priorities and contractual risk.

Understand the whole offer

Weigh purchase price alongside financing, contingencies, concessions, deadlines, possession and the buyer's ability to close.

Negotiate intelligently

Use context, market conditions and contract terms to evaluate counteroffers, inspection discussions, appraisal questions and changes along the way.

Manage the moving parts

Coordinate with the lender, title company and other parties; track key deadlines; and keep you informed through the final steps.
